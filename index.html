Support

StudyPath helps students in grades 6–8 plan homework, practice with adaptive quizzes, and get help from a private on-device study coach.

Need help or have a suggestion?

Email us at venkatesh.aka@gmail.com and we'll reply as soon as we can.

Common questions

How do I change my grade, name, photo, or school?
Tap your profile picture at the top of the app.

Why does the coach say "Offline mode"?
Your device doesn't have Apple Intelligence turned on or doesn't support it. The built-in coach and question bank still work.

Is my information shared?
No. Everything stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy.
