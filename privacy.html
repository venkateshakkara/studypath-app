Privacy Policy

StudyPath: Middle School · Last updated: October 20, 2026

StudyPath does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • No accounts: You don't need to sign up or log in.
  • Data stays on your device: Your name, photo, grade, school details, study plan, notes, and quiz scores are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • On-device AI: The Study Coach and AI Quiz use Apple Intelligence, which runs on your device. Your questions are not sent to us.
  • No tracking or ads: The app has no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking.
  • Links: Some buttons open websites, such as your school's website or the Missouri DESE site, in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.
  • Children: StudyPath is designed for middle school students and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.
  • Deleting your data: Delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.
